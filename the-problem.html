The Problem

A Global Financial Illiteracy Crisis

Financial literacy: a skill that is arguably one of the most important to have in today’s society — yet less than half of all teenagers and adults have it.

Why it matters

The Gap Starts Early, and Compounds

Due to gaps in the education system, limited access to resources, and the complex nature of finance itself, billions of people across the globe struggle with managing their money, leaving them unable to establish financial stability.

At MoneyMind Games, we focus on financial literacy for kids because giving kids early exposure to money management can change the trajectory of their futures. We strive to provide younger generations with opportunities to expand their knowledge of budgeting, investing, and real-world decision-making. By making financial literacy for kids engaging and accessible, we help them enter adulthood with a strong financial foundation to lean on, ultimately setting them up for a lifetime of success.

Financial Illiteracy Costs More Than Money

The problem isn't just about numbers — it's about access, confidence, and opportunity. When kids grow up without financial knowledge, the consequences extend far beyond their bank accounts.

Setbacks Compound

Without budgeting and investing knowledge, young adults are more likely to fall into debt and delay wealth-building, missing out on years of compound growth that can translate into hundreds of thousands of dollars over a lifetime.

Social Consequences

Financial illiteracy disproportionately impacts underserved communities, widening generational wealth gaps while contributing to significant financial stress and anxiety among young adults.

Our solution

We make learning about money actually fun

MoneyMind Games was built by teens who noticed the gap firsthand. Our flagship product, InvestQuest, turns complex financial concepts—portfolios, taxes, risk, and investing—into a fun 2–4 player card game for kids ages 8+. Through classroom showcases, library events, and educator workshops, we make financial literacy engaging and accessible for students and families.
