Our Team

MoneyMind Games was founded in 2025 by high-school students with the goal of teaching financial literacy to kids in an interactive and exciting way.

Our Mission

Building Financial Futures, One Game at a Time

At MoneyMind Games, our mission is to equip the next generation with essential financial literacy skills in a fun, engaging, and accessible way, addressing the issue of a widespread lack of financial literacy. We hope our games can pique players' interest in learning about money at a young age so that they can have the foundation to continue growing their knowledge as they enter adulthood.

At MoneyMind Games, we seek to provide the next generation with a fun, engaging, and accessible way to learn important financial skills. Our goal is to address the educational disparity in each community by providing young people with an equal opportunity to build a strong financial foundation on which they can continue learning and growing as they enter adulthood. Join us in empowering the next generation, one game at a time.
